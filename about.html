About Avena

Who Avena is, how its history is organised, and what its people celebrate.

What Avena is

Avena is a micronation founded on March 5, 2023. It has a government led by a President, a Constitution, a flag, national symbols and its own traditions. Its citizens are called Avenians and its language is English.

Avena is a micronation. This website does not claim international recognition or sovereign status under international law.

Founding Era and Expansion Era

Founding Era

Begins with the founding of Avena on March 5, 2023. The timeline records the founding of Ludis and New Cratia, their annexation, and the adoption of the Constitution on February 8, 2025.

Expansion Era

Began on February 15, 2025. The timeline records Arcanus becoming a temporary capital and fort during the Revolutionary War, and the rediscovery of the Constitution on June 8, 2026.

National identity

Avena's motto is "Viva l'Avena", its national animal is the fox, and its colours are red, green and blue. Read about the national symbols.

Geography and territories

The capital seat is Stonepoint. Avena's territories include Verdan (formerly Treeland), New Ludis, Arcanus and North Pretinum. Only confirmed information is published. Explore the territories.
